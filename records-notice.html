Notice of Automatically Available Records

This notice lists all records held by Proaxis Solutions (Pty) Ltd that are available to the public without the need to submit a formal PAIA request for access.

Published: June 2026
Regulation: PAIA Reg. 5(2)
IO Ref: 2025-058837

Legal Basis: This notice is published in terms of Regulation 5(2) of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA). It lists all categories of records that Proaxis Solutions (Pty) Ltd makes automatically available to any person, without that person having to request access in terms of PAIA.
