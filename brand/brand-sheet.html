Logo Usage Rules
✓ Do
  • Use the SVG/PNG files provided — never recreate manually
  • Use on white, light grey, navy, and teal backgrounds only
  • Maintain clear space of at least the icon height on all sides
  • Use the favicon.svg for browser tabs and app icons
  • Keep wordmark and icon mark together unless space prevents it
✗ Don't
  • Don't recolour, rotate, or distort the logo
  • Don't place on busy photographic backgrounds
  • Don't use drop shadows, outlines, or effects
  • Don't stretch or squeeze the proportions
  • Don't use below 24px height for the full wordmark
