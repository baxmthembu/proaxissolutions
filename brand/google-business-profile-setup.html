Step-by-step — click-by-click setup instructions
Verification note: Google will ask you to verify the business. For a service-area business without a shopfront, verification is usually done by phone call or video recording. Have your phone ready. The call comes to the number you listed above (066 213 6607).
  1. Go to business.google.com Sign in with your Google account. Click the blue "Manage now" or "Add your business" button.
  2. Enter business name: "Proaxis Solutions" Type exactly as shown. If Google suggests an existing listing, make sure it is genuinely yours before claiming it.
  3. Select business category: "Software Company" Start typing "Software" and select "Software Company" from the dropdown. This is the primary category — the most important selection you make.
  4. Add your location Enter: 1 Shantally Close, Milarina Gardens, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. If you don't want the street address public, tick "I also serve customers outside this location" and hide the address after saving.
  5. Set service area: "KwaZulu-Natal" + "South Africa" Add both — this tells Google you serve clients beyond your immediate address.
  6. Add contact details Phone: 066 213 6607 · Website: https://proaxissolutions.co.za
  7. Complete verification Choose phone or video verification when prompted. Answer the call to 066 213 6607 or follow the video instructions. This may take 24–72 hours for Google to confirm.
  8. After verification — add the business description Go to "Edit profile → About → Description". Paste the 680-character description from this document exactly.
  9. Add services Go to "Edit profile → Services". Add each of the 7 services listed above with their descriptions.
  10. Add secondary categories Go to "Edit profile → More info → Categories". Add: Computer Consultant, Business Management Consultant, Internet Marketing Service.
  11. Upload the logo as your profile photo Go to "Add photos → Logo". Upload brand/logo-icon-512.png from your proaxissolutions folder.
  12. Upload a cover photo Go to "Add photos → Cover". Upload brand/logo-full-1200.png as the cover image.
  13. Set opening hours Monday – Friday: 08:00 – 17:00. Saturday + Sunday: Closed.
  14. Confirm everything looks correct and publish Review all fields. Hit Save. Your listing goes live once Google indexes it — usually within 1–3 days after verification.
