**Option B — Written Request**
Submit a written request addressed to the Information Officer at the contact details in Part 1 of this manual. The written request must include:

1. The requestor's full name, contact details, and postal/email address for delivery of the response;

## PART 5 — GROUNDS FOR REFUSAL OF ACCESS

Access to records may be refused on the grounds set out in Chapter 4 of PAIA. [VERIFY: current provisions.] These grounds include, but are not limited to:

| Ground | Description |
|---|---|
| Protection of personal information of third parties | Where disclosure would unreasonably disclose personal information of a third party |
| Protection of commercial information | Where records contain trade secrets or confidential commercial information |
| Protection of safety of individuals | Where disclosure could endanger the life or safety of any person |
| Protection of privileged legal communications | Legally privileged communications with an attorney |
| Protection of research information | Premature disclosure of unpublished research |
| Records not held | Where the record does not exist or is not held by Proaxis Solutions |
| Manifestly frivolous or vexatious requests | Requests made in bad faith or to harass |

Where access is refused, the Information Officer will provide written reasons for the refusal, referencing the applicable PAIA provision.
