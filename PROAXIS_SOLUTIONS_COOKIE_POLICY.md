## WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device on return visits and to store certain information about your preferences or actions.

Cookies are not programs and cannot carry viruses or access information on your device beyond what they were designed to store.

### How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

- View cookies currently stored on your device;
- Block cookies from specific websites;
- Delete cookies at any time;
- Set preferences for future cookies.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect the functioning of this website, including the ability to submit contact forms.

### Browser Cookie Settings

Guidance on managing cookies in common browsers:

- **Google Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data

- **Microsoft Edge:** Settings > Cookies and site permissions
- **Safari (iOS/macOS):** Settings > Safari > Privacy & Security
