Part 7

How to Submit a Request for Access to Records

Any person may request access to records held by Proaxis Solutions by submitting a request to the Information Officer. Requests may be submitted in writing by email or post. Alternatively, a completed Form C (as prescribed by the PAIA Regulations) may be submitted — Form C is available from the Information Regulator at www.inforegulator.org.za.

Every request must include: the requestor's full name and contact details; a description of the record requested; the preferred form of access (inspection, electronic copy, photocopy); and, if requesting on behalf of another person, proof of that authority.

7.1 Access Fees

Access fees are payable as prescribed by the PAIA Regulations. The Information Officer will notify the requestor of any applicable fees before processing the request. Fees are not charged for access to the requestor's own personal information.

7.2 Response Timelines

Proaxis Solutions will acknowledge receipt of a request within 5 business days and provide a decision within the timeframes prescribed by PAIA (typically 30 days from receipt, extendable by a further 30 days where justified).

7.3 Grounds for Refusal

Access may be refused on grounds set out in Chapter 4 of PAIA, including protection of third-party personal information, commercially sensitive information, legally privileged communications, and safety of individuals. Written reasons referencing the applicable PAIA provision will be provided for any refusal.

7.4 Remedies if Access is Refused

A requestor who is dissatisfied with a decision may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (www.inforegulator.org.za) or approach a court with jurisdiction to review the decision.
