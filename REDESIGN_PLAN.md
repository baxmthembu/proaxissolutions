## 4. VISUAL DESIGN SPECIFICATION

- **Primary Navy:** #0F172A (Trust, professionalism)
- **Electric Blue:** #2563EB (Innovation, CTAs)
- **Teal Accent:** #14B8A6 (Automation, growth)

- **Font Family:** Inter

### Layout Principles
1. **Generous Whitespace** - not cramped
2. **Clear Visual Hierarchy** - headlines stand out
3. **Minimal Decorative Elements** - function over form
4. **Data Visualization** - dashboards, workflow diagrams
5. **Consistent Component System** - cards, buttons, CTAs

## 9. AUTOMATION OPPORTUNITY ASSESSMENT (Engagement Tool)

**Positioning:** Professional, data-driven tool—not a gimmick

**Purpose:** Help prospects understand potential automation gains

**Interface Approach:** 
- Minimalist, professional design
- 3-5 targeted questions (not overwhelming)
- Results delivered via email (PDF report)
- Tone: Consultative, business-focused

**Questions to Ask:**
1. "How many hours per week are spent on manual processes in your organization?"
2. "How many team members are involved in these manual tasks?"
3. "How frequently do these processes occur?" (Daily, Weekly, Monthly)
4. "Which department/function is most affected?" (Operations, Admin, Finance, HR, etc.)
5. "What's the estimated annual cost of these manual processes?" (Optional, for context)

**Output (PDF Report):**
- Estimated time savings potential
- Estimated cost reduction opportunity
- Automation complexity assessment
- Recommended next steps
- Invitation to consultation

**Placement:** Floating widget on key pages (Services, How We Work) - not intrusive

**Design Language:** Data-driven, clean, professional (no games or tricks)
