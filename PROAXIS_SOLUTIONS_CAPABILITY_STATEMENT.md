## WHAT WE DO

Proaxis Solutions is a South African **technology, software engineering, and AI automation company**. We design, build, and deploy digital solutions that help organisations automate operations, improve service delivery, and achieve measurable business outcomes.

We work across three core capability areas:

---

### 1. SOFTWARE ENGINEERING

We build custom software that solves real operational problems — from internal workflow systems and business applications to web platforms and integration solutions.

**Capabilities:**
- Custom software development (web applications, business systems, workflow platforms)
- API development and system integration
- Web application development
- Corporate and business website development
- Client portal development
- Platform modernisation

**Suited for:** Organisations that have outgrown spreadsheets and manual processes, or need to replace legacy systems with modern, scalable digital platforms.

---

### 2. AI AUTOMATION

We design and implement AI-powered systems that eliminate repetitive manual work, improve decision-making, and create operational efficiency.

**Capabilities:**
- AI workflow automation
- Intelligent document processing and extraction
- AI assistant development and integration
- Automated reporting and data processing
- Decision-support system design
- Business process automation (BPA)

**Suited for:** Organisations with high-volume, rules-based processes that are currently performed manually, and those seeking to leverage AI to improve quality and reduce operational cost.

> **Our approach to responsible AI:** Every AI solution we design includes a human-in-the-loop mechanism for decisions with significant consequences. We do not design or recommend fully automated decision systems that operate without human review where personal or significant business outcomes are involved. This is embedded in our design practice and in our client contracts.

---

### 3. DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

We help organisations plan and execute the transition from legacy, manual, or fragmented operations to integrated, digital, and data-driven business models.

**Capabilities:**
- Digital transformation strategy and roadmap development
- Technology assessment and gap analysis
- Process mapping and optimisation
- Digital infrastructure advisory
- Change management support
- Project management and delivery oversight

**Suited for:** Organisations at any stage of digital maturity — from businesses digitising for the first time to those modernising existing systems.
