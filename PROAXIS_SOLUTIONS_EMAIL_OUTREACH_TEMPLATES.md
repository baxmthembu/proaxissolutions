## SENDING PROTOCOL — TO AVOID SPAM FLAGS

Since these are now three distinct, fully personalised emails (different subject lines, different bodies, different recipients), the main spam risk isn't duplicate content — it's sending behaviour. Follow this:

1. **Send manually, one at a time**, from your normal email client (Outlook/Gmail web or app) — not a bulk mail-merge tool. Mail-merge headers are a common spam trigger.
2. **Space them across different days**, not back-to-back in one sitting: e.g. Siza's on Monday, Keith's Panel Shop on Wednesday, Ash's Nail Bar on Friday.
3. **Vary send times** — late morning or early afternoon tends to perform best for SME owners checking email between tasks.
4. **No tracking pixels or link-shorteners** in cold outreach — both increase spam-filter scores. Keep links as plain visible URLs (your footer already does this correctly).
5. **Reply-to consistency** — make sure info@proaxissolutions.co.za has SPF/DKIM configured if not already (worth checking with whoever manages your DNS/Cloudflare), since a domain with no email authentication is more likely to land in spam regardless of content.
6. **Warm sending pattern** — since this is a new business email address, sending a handful of personalised emails per week (not dozens per day) also protects your domain's sending reputation while it's still new.
