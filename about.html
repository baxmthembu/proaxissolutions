Proaxis Solutions is a South African technology company on a mission to make intelligent software and automation accessible to every organisation that's ready to grow.

Our Mission

We believe great technology should drive real business outcomes — not just look impressive.

Proaxis Solutions was founded to bridge the gap between technology capability and business reality. Too often, organisations invest in tools that don't fit how they work, or rely on manual processes long past the point where automation would serve them better.

We fix that. We work closely with our clients to understand their operations, design solutions around their specific needs, and build systems that genuinely improve how they work.

Our approach is grounded in software engineering discipline, automation-first thinking, and a commitment to long-term partnership. We don't just deliver a project and disappear — we invest in your outcomes.

How We Work

Discover. Design. Automate. Scale.

Our four-phase methodology ensures every engagement starts with understanding and ends with measurable results.

01

Discover

We go deep into your operations, workflows, and goals before recommending anything. Understanding your business is step one.

02

Design

We design solutions that fit your business logic — not generic products force-fitted to your processes. Architecture and approach are tailored from the start.

03

Automate

We implement with precision — reducing manual work, eliminating bottlenecks, and building systems that run reliably at scale.

04

Scale

We support your growth. As your business evolves, our systems and partnerships evolve with it — maintained, optimised, and ready for what's next.
