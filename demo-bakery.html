Our Story

Baked with purpose, since 2016

Sugar & Crumb was started in a home kitchen in Cape Town by two sisters who believed every person deserves a beautiful, delicious cake — not just for the wealthy.

Today, we serve hundreds of families, corporate clients, and brides across South Africa — and we still bake every single cake by hand.
