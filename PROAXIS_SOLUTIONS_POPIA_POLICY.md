## 10. DATA BREACH RESPONSE PROCEDURE

Proaxis Solutions has established the following procedure for responding to data breaches in accordance with POPIA Section 22. [VERIFY: current Section 22 requirements and notification obligations.]

### Step 1 — Detection and Containment
Upon discovery of a potential breach: immediately contain the breach; isolate affected systems or data; preserve evidence.

### Step 2 — Assessment (within 24 hours of discovery)
Determine: what information was affected; how many data subjects are affected; the likely consequences; whether the breach is ongoing.

### Step 3 — Notification to Information Officer (within 24 hours)
The person who discovers the breach must notify the Information Officer (Andile Khoza, Reference: 2025-058837) immediately.

### Step 4 — Notification to Information Regulator (as soon as reasonably practicable)
The Information Officer must notify the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably practicable after becoming aware of the breach. [VERIFY: current notification requirements, timelines, and notification form — www.inforegulator.org.za]
