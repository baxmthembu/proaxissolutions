⚡ The Short Version

Here's what you actually need to know

We only collect your name, email, and business details when you contact us — nothing else.
We use your information only to respond to your enquiry and deliver our services.

Section 1

Who We Are

This Privacy Notice is issued by Proaxis Solutions (Pty) Ltd, a technology company registered in South Africa (Registration No. 2025/542019/07). We provide custom software development, AI automation, digital transformation, and IT support services.

We are the Responsible Party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) — meaning we decide how and why your personal information is processed.

Section 2

What We Collect and Why

We only collect personal information that is necessary for a specific, legitimate purpose. Here is exactly what we collect:

InformationWhy We Collect ItLegal Basis
Name & email address To respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about our services Consent / Legitimate interest
Business name & industry To understand your context and provide a relevant, tailored response Consent / Contractual necessity

We do not collect special personal information (race, health, religion, biometrics, etc.) and we do not collect information about children.

Section 3

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information strictly for the following purposes:

— To respond to your enquiry or service request
— To prepare and send you a proposal or quote
— To deliver and manage the services you have engaged us for
— To comply with our legal obligations (e.g. tax, contract records)
— To improve our website and communication based on how people engage with us

We will never use your personal information for unsolicited marketing without your explicit consent. If you have opted in to updates from us, you can opt out at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any email.

Section 4

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate — but only where necessary and only under strict confidentiality obligations:

Section 5

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information only for as long as necessary:

— Enquiry data (contact form submissions): 12 months from your last contact with us
— Client project data: Duration of the contract plus 5 years (as required by law)
— Financial records (invoices, payments): 7 years (SARS requirement)
— Website usage data: 12 months

After the applicable retention period, your information is permanently deleted or anonymised.

Section 6

Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the following rights regarding your personal information. You can exercise any of these rights by contacting our Information Officer (details in Section 7).

👁️

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

✏️

Right to Correction

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information about you.

🗑️

Right to Deletion

Request that we delete your personal information where there is no longer a lawful reason to keep it.

🚫

Right to Object

Object to the processing of your information, including for direct marketing purposes.

📦

Right to Portability

Request your information in a structured, commonly used format.

⏸️

Right to Restrict

Ask us to limit how we use your information while a dispute is resolved.

We will respond to all requests within 5 business days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Section 8

Cookies & Website Tracking

Our website uses minimal, functional cookies — small text files stored on your device that help the site work properly.

— Essential cookies: Required for the site to function (e.g. remembering your consent preference). Cannot be disabled.
— Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. Only set with your consent.

You can manage or withdraw your cookie consent at any time using the settings in your browser. Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of any processing done before withdrawal.

Section 9

How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse — including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular security reviews.

In the unlikely event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA Section 22.

Section 10

Changes to This Notice

We review this Privacy Notice annually and whenever our practices change. The current version will always be available on this page with the effective date shown at the top. We will notify active clients of any material changes by email.
